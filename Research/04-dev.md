# Dev

Profoundly deaf, cochlear implant. Software engineer. Uses Cool Captions for
one-to-ones and the occasional pub. Paying subscriber, 8 months. Also tried two
competing apps.

---

**Interviewer:** You've used a few of these apps. What made you settle on this one?

**Dev:** Accuracy, mostly. In a quiet room it's the best of the ones I tried. In a pub
they're all bad, yours included, but yours is bad in a more readable way. It at least
gets whole sentences wrong rather than individual words, if that makes sense.

**Interviewer:** It does. What about the first-run experience?

**Dev:** Fine. The microphone permission thing is a bit abrupt, it asks before you've
seen anything, so you don't know why. I'd put it after the first screen. But I'm an
engineer, I know what it's for. My mum would've said no and then wondered why it
didn't work.

**Interviewer:** Is there anything you've seen in the other apps that you miss here?

**Dev:** One of them highlights the word it's currently on, so you get a sense of
pace. That's quite nice. And one of them had a little waveform thing at the bottom
showing the mic was picking up sound, which I liked more than I expected. You could see
it was alive, even when nobody was talking.

**Interviewer:** Why did that matter?

**Dev:** I think because with captions you're always half a step behind, so there's
this low-level question of whether it's keeping up. The waveform answered that without
me having to think about it. I don't know that I'd have asked for it. But I noticed
when I switched to yours and it wasn't there.

**Interviewer:** Would you want it here?

**Dev:** Maybe. I'd want it to be subtle. The other app's was a bit much, it was
bouncing around the whole time. Something you could ignore until you needed it.
