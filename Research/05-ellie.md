# Ellie

Hard of hearing, university student. Uses Cool Captions in lectures and seminars, two
or three hours a day during term. Paying subscriber via a disability support
allowance, 6 months.

---

**Interviewer:** Lectures are a long time to be reading captions. How do you find it?

**Ellie:** It's tiring, but it's that or nothing, so. The lecturers won't wear the
university mic half the time. I sit at the front and put the phone on the desk.

**Interviewer:** Does it hold up for a whole lecture?

**Ellie:** Usually. The lecture theatre Wi-Fi is awful, though, and I'm on data when
it drops, and then it'll go. I had one where it cut out for a good two minutes and I
didn't clock it, because he was writing on the board and I thought he'd just stopped
talking. He hadn't. He'd been explaining the thing on the board. I got that from a
friend's notes afterwards.

**Interviewer:** How did you feel when you realised?

**Ellie:** Annoyed with myself more than the app, at first. Like I should've been
watching it. But then, that's the thing, isn't it, I shouldn't have to watch it. I
should be watching him.

**Interviewer:** Has it changed how you use it?

**Ellie:** I check it more. I'll glance down every so often just to see if it's still
going. Which means I'm not looking at the slides. And I've started stopping and
starting it between sections, just to, I don't know, reset it. I don't think that does
anything. It's superstition. But it makes me feel like it's fresh.

**Interviewer:** Do you ever get a warning or anything when it drops?

**Ellie:** No. Nothing. It just stops moving. I think once I had a thing come up
saying no connection, but that might have been a different app. I'm not sure.

**Interviewer:** If it did warn you, what would you want it to say?

**Ellie:** Just that it's dropped, really. And then when it's back, that it's back, and
maybe how much I missed, so I know whether to ask him or just let it go. If it's ten
seconds I'll let it go. If it's two minutes I need to know that.
