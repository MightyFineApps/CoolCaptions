# Priya

Deaf since childhood. Uses Cool Captions daily for work meetings, usually on her phone
propped against her laptop. Paying subscriber, 14 months.

---

**Interviewer:** You said earlier that meetings are the main thing. Can you walk me
through a typical one?

**Priya:** So I'll have the app on the phone and the call on the laptop. I'm watching
both. Mostly it's fine, honestly. The captions are good enough that I can keep up, and
I can glance at someone's face when I need to.

**Interviewer:** Mostly fine. What's the not-fine part?

**Priya:** It stops. Not often, but when it does it's horrible, because I don't know
it's stopped. The last line just sits there. And in a meeting people pause anyway, so
I'm thinking, okay, has he finished, is he thinking, is someone else about to talk. And
then it's been ten seconds and I realise, no, the app's gone.

**Interviewer:** What do you do when that happens?

**Priya:** I hit stop and then start again. Sometimes it comes back, sometimes it
doesn't, so I do it again. I've done it three or four times in a row before. Which is
me, sat in a meeting, stabbing at my phone, while everyone's talking. And then when it
does come back I've missed however long that was, and I have to decide whether to ask
someone to repeat it, which I hate doing.

**Interviewer:** Do you know why it stops?

**Priya:** No idea. Wi-Fi, probably? The office Wi-Fi is bad. But I don't actually
know, and that's sort of the point. The app doesn't tell me anything. It just goes
quiet, and quiet could mean anything.

**Interviewer:** If it could tell you one thing in that moment, what would it be?

**Priya:** Whether it's me or it. Like, is it still listening. If I knew it had
dropped I'd just put my hand up and say give me a sec. It's the not knowing that's the
problem. The ten seconds where I'm guessing.
