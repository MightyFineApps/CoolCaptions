# Marta

Mild hearing loss, uses Cool Captions a few times a week in cafés and at family dinners.
Paying subscriber, 3 months. Came to the interview with a list.

---

**Interviewer:** You said you had some things you wanted to raise.

**Marta:** Yes, sorry, I wrote them down. The main one is the text size. I have it on
the biggest setting and it's still a bit small for me at arm's length, and I don't want
to hold the phone up to my face at dinner. It makes people self-conscious.

**Interviewer:** Noted. What else?

**Marta:** Dark mode. At dinner the screen is like a torch. I know I can turn the
brightness down but then I can't read it. A proper dark background would help.

**Interviewer:** Anything about the captions themselves?

**Marta:** They're good. Names are a bit hit and miss, it keeps writing my
son-in-law's name as something else. And sometimes it lags, like it's a few seconds
behind, but then it catches up, so that's fine. I've learned not to worry about that.

**Interviewer:** Does the lag change how you use it?

**Marta:** Not really. You just wait. Everyone's talking over each other at dinner
anyway, so it's not like I'm getting every word regardless. It's the gist I'm after.

**Interviewer:** And sharing, you had that on the list?

**Marta:** Yes. My daughter wanted to see what the app had got from a conversation
and I couldn't work out how to send it. I ended up screenshotting it. Is there a way?

**Interviewer:** There is, it's under the share button on the saved transcript. We can
look at making that more obvious.

**Marta:** That'd be good. I didn't even know it saved them.
