# Tom

Hard of hearing, late onset. Uses Cool Captions mainly at medical appointments with his
father, who has dementia, so Tom needs the transcript afterwards as a record. Free
tier, considering upgrading.

---

**Interviewer:** You mentioned the appointments. How does the app fit into those?

**Tom:** I put the phone on the desk between us and the doctor. I'm not really reading
it live, to be honest, I can hear well enough in a quiet room. It's more that afterwards
I can go back through what she said, because Dad won't remember and I'll have only half
of it.

**Interviewer:** So it's the transcript afterwards that matters.

**Tom:** Yes. And that's where I got caught out. There was one where we got home and I
went to check what she'd said about the dosage, and there was a chunk missing. Maybe a
minute. The transcript just went from one thing to something unrelated, and the bit I
wanted was in between.

**Interviewer:** Did you notice anything at the time?

**Tom:** No. That's what annoyed me. If I'd known in the room I'd have asked her to say
it again. I did glance at it a couple of times and I suppose it wasn't moving, but I
thought she'd just paused, or I'd missed her talking. I wasn't watching it closely. Why
would I? It was recording.

**Interviewer:** Was it recording?

**Tom:** Well, evidently not. Or it was and it lost it. I don't know which. There was
no error, nothing. It just carried on as if nothing had happened, and the gap was only
there when I looked for it later.

**Interviewer:** How did that affect how you use it?

**Tom:** I don't trust it for the important ones now. I take notes as well, which
rather defeats the point. I'd still pay for it, I think, if I could be sure it had
everything. It's a good app. But I need to know when it hasn't got everything, and I
need to know at the time, not in the car on the way home.
